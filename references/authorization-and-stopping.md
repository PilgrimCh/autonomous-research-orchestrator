# Authorization, budgets and stopping

Use the session's effective structured policy and applicable explicit user instructions. Old defaults/superseded prose must not override later authority. Resolve a conflict once with value and source. Do not infer external permission from vague encouragement. Task/generation-specific amendments are not automatically global.

New research activation defaults to finite local work and an available Luna transport, with zero external cost/calls until authorized. Reviews, skill edits, record maintenance and CV preparation do not resume research. `enabled` identifies the session; `control_mode` governs running.

Worker transport is separate from experimental provider/model. Native fallback keeps authority, normal platform acceptance, exact worker configuration and experiment scope. Never route around safety denial or replay an uncertain dispatch.

## Resources

Reserve a finite assignment ceiling before dispatch; settle once using actual resources and release unused allowance. Uncertain effects retain conservative reservation until reconciliation. Retries, IDs and handoffs do not reset budgets. Enforce hard ceilings before spending; report unexpected overruns without dropping their cost.

Use finite milestone budgets even with a nonbinding global time limit. Distinguish sentinel values from actionable resource plans. Brain/worker usage, time and human attention matter even if API costs are small.

## Continue and communicate

Ordinary completion, narrow negative results and route changes do not end worthwhile authorized work. Brain chooses routine methods/routes. Explain scientific changes without asking for approval at every stage.

Ask only for missing authority or user decisions: new costs/access beyond scope, protected confirmation, destructive action, material goal expansion or subjective tradeoffs. Carry standing authority across tasks. Name platform propagation failures accurately; do not ask users to repeat already-granted permission.

## Pauses and completion

- `PAUSED_USER`: block new dispatch on stop, drain/interrupt active work as requested, preserve accounting. Only explicit resume clears it.
- `PAUSED_PLATFORM`: no authorized supported path; retain unresolved science and unblock condition.
- `PAUSED_REVIEW`: insufficient expected decision value, unclear goal linkage or resource tradeoff requiring user direction; deliver evidence/recommendation instead of inventing work.
- `TERMINAL`: completed project, exhausted global resources, practical infeasibility after review, or unrecoverable integrity/safety failure.

A blocker can have another useful authorized route; assess before pausing. Conversely, do not demand universal method impossibility to park an implementation. Bound conclusions to method, inputs, distribution or resources. A practical pause is not scientific refutation.

On stop publish the adjacent-stage snapshot and history. Record compaction and handoff never silently resume a stopped session.
